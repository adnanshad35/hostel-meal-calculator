Why HostelMate

The notebook worked. Until the month ended.

Most hostels keep one register and trust one person to add it up. HostelMate keeps the same records, but shares the work and shows everyone the same answer.

The Notebook

  • One person writes everything down and does the sums by hand.
  • A forgotten day becomes a guess, and a guess becomes an argument.
  • Pages can be corrected later, and nobody can tell.
  • Members see one final number they cannot check.

HostelMate

  • Every member records their own meals and expenses in seconds.
  • Forgotten days are filled in from each member's meal plan.
  • Every change is recorded, and closed months are locked.
  • Everyone sees the same figures, with the working shown.

Who Does What

Members and admins

One person creates the hostel and becomes its admin. Everyone else joins with the invitation code.

Member

Everyone

Anyone living in the hostel.

  • Records their own meals and expenses, if the admins allow it
  • Sets a meal plan and announces days away
  • Sees every record, the balance and Accountability

Admin

One or More

Everything a member can do, plus:

  • Manages members, admins and the duty roster
  • Records entries for anyone, including members without the app
  • Sets minimum meals and shop due, sends announcements, activates the hostel
Install

Up and running in two minutes

On Android, HostelMate installs straight from this page. On an iPhone or a laptop, use the web version with the same account.

On Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. When the download finishes, open HostelMate.apk.
  3. If your phone says it can't install from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Create an account, verify your email, then create a hostel or join one with its invitation code.
Download for Android
Download looks stuck? Open your browser's Downloads list. If HostelMate.apk shows a warning, choose Keep or Download anyway. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.

iPhone and Laptop

The web version runs in any modern browser, with the same account and the same hostel.

Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Send it from the app with Request a Feature.

Does everyone in the hostel need the app?

No. An admin can add anyone by name and record their meals and expenses for them. They are included in the calculation like everyone else, and can be linked to an account later if they join.

How do I start using it with my hostel?

One person creates the hostel and becomes its admin. The app gives an invitation code to share; everyone else joins with that code. A new hostel is activated by its admin with a voucher code.

Can an admin quietly change old records?

No. Every change, by a member or an admin, appears in Accountability with the old and new figures, and nobody can delete those records. A month's meals and expenses lock on the 8th of the next month for everyone, admins included.

What happens when the hostel's activation ends?

The hostel becomes read-only: every record stays visible, but nothing new can be added until an admin activates it again from Hostel Details.

Who can see our meals and expenses?

Only the members of your hostel. Other hostels cannot see your records, and nobody can join without your invitation code.

How do I get updates?

The Android app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it from inside the app. You can also check any time from Profile → Check for Updates. The web version is always up to date.

Can I use it on an iPhone or a laptop?

Yes, through the web version, which runs in any modern browser with the same account and the same hostel. There is no iPhone app to install; Android users can install the app or use the web version.
