How it works

From daily entries to one fair amount

Three small habits replace the notebook and the end-of-month debate.

1

Record your meals

Tap +, set lunch and dinner, save. It takes a few seconds, and every member does their own.

2

Add what you spent

Bought rice or paid the gas bill? Add the expense and the whole hostel is notified.

3

Settle in one tap

At month end the app shows what each person pays or collects. Copy it straight into your group chat.

Features

Everything a shared kitchen needs

Built around how hostels actually run.

An automatic, transparent calculation

No spreadsheet and no trust required. The same figures appear on every member's phone, with the working shown.

Meal rate = Total cost ÷ Total meals
You pay = Your meals × Meal rate − What you spent

Meal plans and a duty roster

Set your usual lunch and dinner once, and days you forget are still counted. Admins choose who looks after the hostel on which dates, shown on everyone's home screen.

Members without the app

Add someone by name. An admin records their meals, and they are included in every calculation.

Announcements and alerts

Admins post short notices to the hostel. Members are told whenever a new expense is added.

Private to your hostel

Only your hostel's members can see its records, and nobody joins without your invitation code.

Install

Up and running in two minutes

On Android, HostelMate is installed directly from this page, so your phone asks for permission once. On anything else, open the web version.

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. When the download finishes, open HostelMate.apk.
  3. If your phone says it can't install from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Create an account, verify your email, then create a hostel or join one with its invitation code.
Download for Android
Download looks stuck? Open your browser's Downloads list. If HostelMate.apk shows a warning, choose Keep or Download anyway. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.
On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera to open this page there.

Questions

Good to know

Does everyone in the hostel need the app?

No. An admin can add anyone by name and record their meals and expenses for them. They are included in the calculation like everyone else, and can be linked to an account later if they join.

How do I start using it with my hostel?

One person creates the hostel and becomes its admin. The app gives an invitation code to share; everyone else joins with that code. A new hostel is activated by its admin with a voucher code.

Who can see our meals and expenses?

Only the members of your hostel. Other hostels cannot see your records, and nobody can join without your invitation code.

Can I use it on an iPhone or a laptop?

Yes, through the web version, which runs in any modern browser with the same account and the same hostel. There is no iPhone app to install; Android users can install the app or use the web version.
